### 1. Independent Plugins

For the purposes of this exception, an "Independent Plugin" is a separately developed software module that interacts with GakuLoader through its documented public plugin interfaces.

An Independent Plugin may be distributed as a dynamically loaded library and may exchange data, callbacks, function pointers, and service interfaces with GakuLoader through these public interfaces.
